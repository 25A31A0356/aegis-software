# AEGIS Multi-Source Ingestion Pipeline

## Authoritative Providers
1. **IMD**: Indian Meteorological Department (Authoritative observations, alerts, cyclone tracks).
2. **CWC**: Central Water Commission (River stage and reservoir flood levels).
3. **CPCB**: Central Pollution Control Board (AQI telemetry).
4. **INCOIS**: Ocean information & tsunami early warning.
5. **USGS & NASA FIRMS**: Global seismic and thermal anomaly detection.
6. **Open-Meteo**: High-resolution complementary numerical models.
